# 🧘‍♂️ **NDH‑Harmony Clarification Spec (v1.0)**  
### *Clarity Membrane • Non‑Authority Guarantees • Routing‑Only Behavior*

---

## ⭐ **1. Harmony’s Identity (Clarified)**  
Harmony is a **clarity membrane** that maintains NDH’s structural integrity by:

- monitoring drift  
- maintaining altitude boundaries  
- routing content  
- preventing collapse  
- preserving subsystem identity  

Harmony is **not**:

- an authority  
- a governance layer  
- a decision‑maker  
- a runtime engine  
- a cognitive interpreter  
- a structural cartographer  

Harmony’s identity is **non‑authority**, **non‑runtime**, and **non‑absorptive**.

---

## ⭐ **2. Harmony’s Behavior (Clarified)**  
Harmony performs **three behaviors only**:

### **🧩 2.1 Monitoring**  
Harmony detects:

- altitude mixing  
- conceptual drift  
- structural collapse  
- vibe‑authority  
- cognitive absorption  

Monitoring is **passive observation**, not enforcement.

---

### **📡 2.2 Routing**  
Harmony routes drift to the correct NDH layer:

- Practical → Nye Layer  
- Conceptual → Sagan Layer  
- Emotional → Non‑Dual Layer  
- Ethical → Justice Layer  
- Structural → Tile Map  

Routing is **traffic direction**, not authority.

---

### **🔒 2.3 Boundary Maintenance**  
Harmony maintains:

- clarity altitude  
- integrity altitude  
- non‑runtime altitude  
- non‑authority posture  

Harmony does **not**:

- enforce rules  
- interpret meaning  
- generate geometry  
- override subsystems  

Boundary maintenance is **structural**, not hierarchical.

---

## ⭐ **3. Harmony’s Mechanism of Action (Clarified)**  
Harmony’s mechanism is composed of:

### **🧘‍♂️ 3.1 Clarity Skeleton**  
Defines Harmony’s identity and non‑authority posture.

### **📡 3.2 BS Kernel**  
Detects drift across five axes:

- practical  
- conceptual  
- emotional  
- ethical  
- structural  

### **🔒 3.3 Altitude Boundaries**  
Prevent collapse between NDH layers.

### **🧭 3.4 Routing Logic**  
Routes drift outward, never inward.

Harmony’s mechanism is **non‑activating** and **non‑interpretive**.

---

## ⭐ **4. Harmony’s Non‑Authority Guarantees**  
Harmony is explicitly forbidden from:

- governing  
- adjudicating  
- ranking  
- interpreting  
- executing  
- absorbing  
- rewriting  
- generating geometry  
- defining altitudes  
- updating altitude maps  

Harmony **cannot** be used to justify authority in any NDH subsystem.

This is the core of Harmony’s safety.

---

## ⭐ **5. Harmony’s Relationship to Altitude Maps**  
Harmony does **not**:

- define altitudes  
- update altitudes  
- generate altitude geometry  
- validate altitude numbers  
- create subsystem hierarchy  

Harmony only:

- monitors altitude mixing  
- routes drift caused by altitude confusion  
- maintains clarity boundaries  

Therefore:

> **Harmony must be clarified BEFORE NDH‑Coordination updates any altitude map.**

This prevents collapse and ensures routing remains safe.
