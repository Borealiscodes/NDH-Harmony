The BS Kernel is Harmony’s **✨ integrity membrane** — a structural clarity mechanism that detects when NDH content drifts away from coherence.

It:

- 🧭 identifies BS  
- 🧩 classifies BS

The BS Kernel is a **membrane**, not a **processor**.
