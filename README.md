## ⭐ **1. What NDH‑Harmony Is**  
NDH‑Harmony is the **clarity‑and‑integrity subsystem** of the NDH ecosystem.  
It is the layer responsible for:

- 🧩 **structural coherence**  
- ✨ **altitude separation**  
- 📡 **routing of drift**  
- 🔒 **non‑authority guarantees**  
- 🛡️ **membrane protection against cognitive‑mode artifacts**  

Harmony is **not** a cognitive engine, not a governance layer, not a vibe‑authority, and not a runtime system.  
It is a **clarity skeleton** — a stable, non‑absorptive structure that keeps NDH architectures aligned and intact.

Harmony’s job is simple and profound:

> **Maintain clarity. Maintain boundaries. Maintain integrity.**

Everything else routes outward.
